Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who uses Jarvis

Jarvis is a private personal assistant with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to the public, and no other person's Google account is connected to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, Jarvis requests read-only access to the owner's own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs. It cannot send email, create or change events, or modify, share or delete files.

How the data is used

Data is read only when the owner asks a question, to answer that question. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Content needed to answer a question is processed by the AI model that powers the assistant, solely to produce the answer.

Storage

The OAuth token is stored encrypted on the owner's private server. Google data is not copied into any other database; temporary files are deleted after use.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in this site's repository.
