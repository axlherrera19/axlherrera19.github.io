Jarvis

Jarvis is a private, single-user personal assistant run by its owner for his own use. It is not offered to the public and no one else can sign in to it.

With the owner's consent, it reads (read-only) his own Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs to answer his questions. It never sends, modifies or deletes anything in Google.

Jarvis es un asistente personal privado, de un solo usuario, que su dueño usa para sí mismo. No se ofrece al público. Lee en solo lectura su Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets y Docs para responder a sus preguntas, y nunca envía, modifica ni borra nada.
